Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE OF APPROVAL]

The short version: we collect what we need to find and buy tickets for you, and nothing else. We don't sell your information or share it for advertising. We never see your full card number. With Google Calendar we see only free/busy times, never what your events are.

1. What we collect

You give us:

  • Contact details: your phone number (from your first text), your name and email address (at setup), and your ZIP code.
  • Preferences: teams, artists, comedians and shows you follow; genres and event types; how many people usually go with you; your budget range; travel range; whether you drive; when you're usually free for shows; and your swap and alert settings.
  • Messages: the texts you send Billett and the ones we send you. If you text a photo, our messaging provider keeps it, and we save the link to it with your message.
  • Screenshots (optional): at setup, you can upload up to 3 screenshots of your music app so we can pick out artists you like. See §3.
  • Orders: what you approved (event, seats, quantity, total, time), what was bought, and delivery details.

Collected automatically:

  • Payment details, through Stripe: Stripe stores your card. We keep only a Stripe customer ID, the card brand and the last 4 digits, plus a record of each hold, charge, release and refund.
  • Calendar (only if you connect it): see §4.
  • Location: we turn your ZIP code into an approximate map point to measure how far events are. We don't use your phone's location.
  • How you use Billett: which alerts you said yes or no to and how quickly. This shapes what we send you (fewer alerts if you rarely say yes, for example).
  • Basic technical data: our hosting provider logs standard request data (IP address, browser type) when you open a setup or calendar link.

From the waitlist: if you joined the waitlist at billett.app, we have the phone number and first name you entered.

2. How we use it

  • To find events you'd like and that you can get to, check prices, and text you offers and alerts.
  • To buy tickets you approve, charge the agreed total, and deliver the tickets.
  • To prevent fraud (for example, reviewing every first order by hand) and to answer payment disputes with the record of what you approved.
  • To improve Billett for you: alert timing and thresholds based on your replies.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting duties.

We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

3. AI (Claude)

We use Claude, an AI model from Anthropic, to:

  • read each text you send, to understand what you're asking for (team, date, number of seats, budget and so on);
  • help write our replies;
  • read the music screenshots you upload at setup.

What Claude receives: the text of your message, a short summary of the conversation so far, and the relevant preferences. Screenshots are held only in memory for the few seconds it takes to read them, and are never saved to our database, disk or logs. We keep only the artist names read from them. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, Anthropic doesn't train its models on data sent through its API.

4. Google Calendar and Apple Calendar

Connecting a calendar is optional.

Google Calendar. If you connect it, we ask Google for two permissions:

  • calendar.freebusy: whether you're free or busy at given times. We never see event titles, descriptions, locations or attendees. We store only the busy time ranges, for the next 60 days, refreshed at most once an hour.
  • calendar.app.created: lets us create one calendar named "Billett" in your account and add the events you book with us. We can't see or change any other calendar.

We store Google's access tokens encrypted. You can disconnect at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by asking us.

Google API disclosure. Billett's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google data only to check whether you're free for an event and to add your bookings to your Billett calendar. We don't use it for advertising, don't sell it, don't let people read it except where needed for security or the law, and don't use it to train AI models.

Apple Calendar. We give you a private feed address that your phone subscribes to. It lists only the events you've booked with Billett. We can't see your Apple calendar.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Stop texts: reply STOP at any time.
  • See, correct or delete your information: email billett.tickets@gmail.com from your setup email, or text us. We'll reply within 30 days. We may need to keep some records after a deletion request: orders and payment records for tax and dispute purposes (§8).
  • Change preferences: text Billett or use your settings link.
  • Disconnect your calendar: as in §4.

California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use and share it, to delete it, to correct it, and to opt out of its sale or sharing (we don't sell or share it). We won't treat you differently for using these rights.

7. Texting

We text only the number you texted us from, about Billett. See Terms §4 for what we send and how to opt out. We don't share your phone number or texting consent with third parties for their marketing.

8. How long we keep it

  • Account and preferences: while your account is open. After you ask us to delete your account, within 30 days.
  • Texts: 2 years, then deleted, so we can answer questions about past orders.
  • Orders, consent records and payment records: 7 years, for tax and dispute purposes.
  • Calendar free/busy: refreshed hourly and kept for the next 60 days only; deleted when you disconnect.
  • Screenshots: never stored.
  • Waitlist: until you join or ask us to remove you.

9. Security

Billett runs on Railway with encrypted connections. Calendar tokens are encrypted at rest, and card details stay with Stripe. Only Billett's operator can reach the admin tools, behind a password. No system is perfectly secure: if a breach affects your information, we'll tell you as the law requires.

10. Children

Billett is for adults 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.
