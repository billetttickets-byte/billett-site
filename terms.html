1. What Billett is

Billett is a ticket concierge you use by text message. You tell us which teams, artists, comedians and shows you follow. We watch resale prices and text you when a game or show you can make looks like a good deal. When you reply YES to a specific offer, a person at Billett buys those tickets for you from a third-party resale marketplace and has them sent to you.

Billett is not the event organizer, the venue, the team or the original ticket seller. Each event is run by its organizer, and your tickets are subject to the organizer's and venue's rules (entry, bag policy, refunds when an event is canceled, and so on).

2. Who can use Billett

  • You must be at least 18 years old and able to make a binding agreement.
  • During the pilot, Billett is open only to people who live in California and give a California ZIP code at setup.
  • You must use your own phone number and a payment card you are authorized to use.
  • One account per person. Don't sign up for someone else without their permission.

We may decline, pause or close an account at any time, for example if a payment looks fraudulent or if the account is used to break these terms.

3. How buying works

  1. Teasers. A deal alert ("teaser") names a game or show and an approximate all-in price per ticket. A teaser is not an offer, and replying to it doesn't buy anything.
  2. Offers. When you ask for tickets, or say yes to a teaser, we send one specific offer or 2–3 numbered options. Each shows the event, section and row, the number of seats, and one all-in total. An offer is good for 10 minutes.
  3. Your YES is your authorization. Replying YES (or the option number) to an offer authorizes Billett to buy those exact seats for you, as your agent, for up to the all-in total shown, and to charge your card for it. We keep a record of each YES: the seats, quantity, total and time.
  4. Card hold. When you say YES, we place a temporary hold on your card for the all-in total. We charge the card only after the tickets are bought. If we can't buy them (sold out, price went up, or not bought within our time limit), we release the hold and you're not charged.
  5. Substitutions. If your seats sell before we can buy them, we may buy comparable seats instead, but only if they're in the same level, within 3 sections, and cost the same or less than you approved. Anything that costs more, or is in a different level, needs a new YES from you.
  6. We never charge more than you approved. If we buy for less than quoted, you pay the lower amount.
  7. Larger orders. Your first order is limited to $750. After that, orders over $2,000 (or over $4,000 in a week) need an extra confirmation.

4. Text messages

  • What we send. Replies to your texts, offers, order updates, delivery notices and, if you follow teams or performers, deal alerts and new-date alerts. Billett sends at most 3 deal alerts a week, and no unprompted texts between 10 pm and 9 am your time (except updates about an order you placed).
  • Consent. By texting Billett and finishing setup, you agree to receive these texts at the number you used. Consent isn't a condition of buying anything.
  • Opt out at any time by replying STOP (also STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, END or QUIT). You'll get one confirmation and nothing else. Reply START to rejoin. Reply HELP for help, or email billett.tickets@gmail.com.
  • Costs. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.
  • Carriers. Texts are sent through our messaging provider (Sendblue), mostly over iMessage, otherwise SMS. Carriers aren't liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

5. Prices and fees

  • Every price we quote is all-in: the ticket price, the marketplace's fees, and Billett's service fee, in one total. Taxes the marketplace charges are included where they apply.
  • Billett's service fee is 7.5% of the ticket cost (with marketplace fees), with a minimum of $3 per order. If the all-in total isn't a whole dollar, we round it down, never up.
  • Parking passes are priced the same way: the pass cost plus the same fee, as one total.
  • Our fee is for the concierge service: watching prices, finding seats, buying and delivering them. It is not a markup on the ticket.

6. Delivery

Most tickets arrive as a mobile transfer to the email address you gave at setup. Accept the transfer in the team's, venue's or ticketing app. Some arrive as a PDF from billett.tickets@gmail.com. We'll text you when they're on the way. You're responsible for giving us a correct email address and accepting transfers before the event.

7. Refunds, cancellations and postponements

See the Refund and Cancellation Policy. In short: a canceled event is refunded in full, Billett's fee included; a postponed event's tickets carry over to the new date; and resale purchases are final otherwise.

8. Parking passes

At some venues, after you book tickets, we may offer an official parking pass. It works like a ticket offer: a separate all-in total, a separate YES, and a separate order. Lot rules, hours and towing are the lot operator's.

9. Calendar

The AI can misread a message: an offer always shows the exact seats and total, and nothing is bought without your YES to that offer.
