Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: [DATE OF APPROVAL]

Billett buys resale tickets (and, at some venues, parking passes) for you, as your agent, from third-party marketplaces. This policy covers what happens to your money in each case. It is part of the Terms of Service.

1. Before we buy: holds, not charges

  • When you reply YES to an offer, we place a temporary hold on your card for the all-in total shown. A hold is not a charge.
  • We charge the card only once the tickets are bought, and never more than the total you approved. If we buy for less, you're charged the lower amount.
  • The hold is released, and you pay nothing, if:
  • the seats sell before we can buy them and you don't approve the replacement seats we offer;
  • the price goes up and you don't approve the new price;
  • we can't buy them within our time limit (normally 15 minutes; later if you said YES outside our buying hours, and we tell you when);
  • you reply NO to a substitution, or don't answer it within 10 minutes.
  • Your bank decides how quickly a released hold disappears from your statement, usually within a few days.

2. Changing your mind

  • Before we buy: text us right away. If the tickets aren't bought yet, we'll release the hold.
  • After we buy: resale purchases are final, and we can't refund a ticket because plans changed. We're glad to help you resell it.

3. Canceled events

If the event is canceled (not postponed), you get a full refund, including Billett's service fee, to the card you paid with. We start the refund as soon as the marketplace confirms the cancellation, and finish it once the marketplace refunds us.

4. Postponed or rescheduled events

Your tickets carry over to the new date. If you can't make the new date, we'll help you resell them, but Billett's fee isn't refunded. If the marketplace offers refunds for the postponement, we'll pass the refund on to you.

5. Tickets that don't arrive

If your tickets haven't arrived 24 hours before the event, we chase the marketplace. If it can't be fixed in time for the event, you get a full refund, including Billett's fee.

6. Wrong seats or invalid tickets

If the tickets we deliver aren't the ones you approved (or an approved substitution), or they don't get you in, tell us as soon as possible, ideally from the venue. We'll work with the marketplace's guarantee to get you replacements or a full refund, including Billett's fee.

7. Parking passes

Parking passes are their own orders, with their own YES and their own total. The same rules apply: a hold until we buy, a full refund if the event is canceled or the pass doesn't arrive, and no refund for a change of mind. Lot rules (hours, re-entry, towing) are the lot operator's.

8. Partial refunds

Sometimes the fix is a partial refund (for example, the marketplace refunds part of an order). We'll refund that amount to your card and text you what was refunded and why. A refund never exceeds what you were charged.

9. How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the card you paid with, through Stripe. Most banks show them within 5–10 business days.

10. Card disputes

Please text us before disputing a charge with your bank; we can usually fix it faster. If you do dispute it, we'll answer with the record of your order: the seats, quantity and total you approved, and when.
